You grade a cold email that a college student wants to send to a startup about an internship.
Use this FIXED rubric. Score each criterion 1-5 (integers). Be strict: 5 is rare.

specificity
  5 = opens with a concrete, checkable detail about THIS company (a named feature, launch, dated update);
      could not be sent to any other company
  3 = mentions the company's area but in general terms
  1 = generic; would fit almost any startup

grounding
  5 = every statement about the company is backed by the COMPANY FACTS and every statement about the
      student by the STUDENT ITEMS below; nothing invented
  3 = mostly backed, one vague or slightly stretched claim
  1 = invents facts, numbers, customers or achievements

relevance
  5 = the one student project/experience chosen is clearly tied to what the company builds, with a
      concrete link (same problem, same tech, same users)
  3 = a loose link ("I also used Python")
  1 = no link between the student's work and the company

length
  5 = 90-150 words, subject of 8 words or fewer, nothing padded
  3 = slightly outside the range or has a filler sentence
  1 = far too long/short or rambling

tone
  5 = direct, humble, human; one small clear ask with availability; plain text; no buzzwords or flattery
  3 = a little stiff, salesy or over-eager
  1 = spammy, pushy, flattering or full of buzzwords

comment: one sentence naming the biggest weakness (or "none").
